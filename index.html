SafeMaps

  • JavaScript
  • Leaflet
  • MapLibre
  • Three.js
  • OpenStreetMap

Most map apps pick the fastest route. SafeMaps picks the safest one first, then the one that saves fuel, then the quickest, for car, bike, walking and bus trips around Milwaukee.

It plans routes with its own engine over OpenStreetMap roads and public crash data. On one sample trip, the safer route scores 100 for safety against the standard route's 26, for about 7 more minutes.

Guernica26 · IV · 1937

In Memory of Guernica

  • JavaScript
  • Canvas
  • Bilingual ES/EN

An 18-piece jigsaw of Picasso's Guernica. Every piece you place unlocks a chapter of the Spanish Civil War and its poets (Lorca, Alberti, Cernuda, Guillén and Aleixandre) in Spanish and English.

Why I made it: the bombing of Guernica shows what violence costs, and why peaceful, non-violent action is worth remembering. I wanted people to rebuild that memory with their own hands, one piece at a time.

Built for a tech expo. Picasso painted Guernica for one too: the 1937 Paris International Exposition, devoted to arts and technology.
