Favicon: the VP monogram from the site header (.monogram in styles.css).

- White "VP" in Merriweather Black (900), letter-spacing -0.02em, on #2e2c55.
- Text is 16/38.4 of the icon size; corners are rounded at 10/38.4 of the size.
- apple-touch-icon.png has square corners, because iOS rounds them itself.
- Font: Merriweather by Sorkin Type, SIL Open Font License (fonts.google.com/specimen/Merriweather).

The same files are used by the SafeMaps demo (safemaps/icons/vp, built from
routes/demo/static/icons) and the Guernica puzzle (guernica/assets/icons).
